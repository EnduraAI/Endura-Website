About Endura

An Australian specialist consultancy for late-life and decommissioning decisions. Direct technical involvement in studies, engineering review and execution strategy.

An independent technical view

Inherited scope and asset records need to be tested against the work that is actually proposed.

Left untested, they can distort scope and cost long before offshore work begins. Endura brings execution experience into the planning stage, while the important decisions are still changeable.

The role is independent and vendor-neutral. Options are tested against the client’s objectives and the physical, marine and disposal constraints of the asset.

Endura Decommissioning is the consultancy offering of Van Iersel Projects Pty Ltd. Specialist inputs are brought into agreed scopes, with clear ownership of the advisory basis.

Why I started Endura

I started Endura after more than 20 years offshore, working alongside the operators whose late-life assets I now help take from liability to closure. As an individual contractor I sat inside teams at Santos, Woodside and Kent, and saw the same problems each time: estimates built on assumption, records scattered across changes of ownership, and provisions that no one on the current team had written. Endura lets me bring that experience to more than one operator, as an independent adviser with no stake in who does the work.

decomm.ai came from the same years. I worked on study after study and ARO estimate after ARO estimate, and watched good engineering disappear once the report was issued. There was no asset register anyone trusted, no record of what had been assumed and no lessons carried into the next job. More than once I redid work someone else had already done, and sometimes work I had done myself. decomm.ai keeps the records, the assumptions and the decisions in one place, so each study starts from the evidence the last one left behind, not a blank page. Engineers still do the thinking. The platform makes sure it isn’t lost.

Jed van IerselDirector, Endura DecommissioningCo-founder, decomm.ai
