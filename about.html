About / Endura Decommissioning

Independent expertise. Direct involvement.

An Australian specialist consultancy for late-life and decommissioning decisions. Direct technical involvement in studies, engineering review and execution strategy.

Why Endura exists

An independent technical view.

Inherited scope and asset records need to be tested against the work that is actually proposed.

These issues can distort scope and cost long before offshore work begins. Endura brings execution experience into the planning stage, while the important decisions are still changeable.

The role is independent and vendor-neutral. Options are tested against the client’s objectives and the physical, marine and disposal constraints of the asset.

Endura Decommissioning is the consultancy offering of Van Iersel Projects Pty Ltd. Specialist inputs are brought into agreed scopes, with clear ownership of the advisory basis.

Leadership

Jed van Iersel

Offshore experience applied to the decisions made before execution.

Jed’s work spans front-end planning, offshore removal, marine interfaces and close-out across international decommissioning environments.

He founded Endura to bring that judgement into studies and planning, where the scope, cost and risk position can still be challenged.
