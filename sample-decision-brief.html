Sample / Offshore module removal

Illustrative content. No actual asset has been assessed.

01 / Purpose and scope

Agree the work needed before selecting a method.

Define the information and engineering work needed to compare removal as one unit with removal in engineered sections.

Scope
One redundant topsides module, from preparation and separation through transport and receiving handover.
Exclusions
Wells, substructure and unrelated systems are outside this example.
Decision sought
Agree the verification and study tasks needed before a preferred removal approach is selected.

Compare on a common basis.

Both approaches address the same scope and end state. No preferred option or calculated ranking is assigned.
