Privacy

Privacy and public enquiry handling.

Last updated: 11 September 2026

This notice explains how information submitted through the public Endura Decommissioning website is handled.

Information collected

The enquiry form collects your name, work email, selected area of interest and message. A company name may also be provided.

Website measurement

No analytics provider is active in this release. Technical interactions expose an event within the page only; it is not sent to an analytics service. The event labels exclude form fields, email addresses, self-check answers, scores, query strings and client records. Any future analytics service requires an updated notice before activation.

Purpose

The information is used to respond to your enquiry, understand the potential scope and arrange an appropriate follow-up.

Form processing

The enquiry form submits the information entered to Formspree, a third-party form-processing service, for delivery to Endura. Formspree processing is subject to its own privacy and security arrangements.

Project information

Do not submit controlled drawings, client records, credentials or sensitive project information through the public form. A secure transfer route can be agreed after initial contact.

Storage and disclosure

Information is retained only as required to respond and manage the business enquiry. It is not sold. It may be disclosed to service providers supporting website hosting or communications where necessary.

Contact and deletion requests

Questions, access requests or deletion requests can be sent to jed@enduradecom.com.

Preferences in your browser

The theme preference is saved locally in this browser until its site data is cleared. Checked items in the asset retirement obligation (ARO) Defensibility Checklist are saved for the current browser session. These preferences and selections are not sent to Endura. The Liability self-check does not transmit answers or save them to application storage. The checklist is available without providing contact details.

Briefing requests

The briefing form collects your name, email address and explicit consent to receive future Endura briefing emails. Formspree processes the request through the existing enquiry endpoint. Endura uses the information to respond to the request and manage permitted briefing correspondence. Consent can be withdrawn by emailing jed@enduradecom.com.

The site does not itself operate an automated mailing platform. A request is not confirmation of a particular publication date.
