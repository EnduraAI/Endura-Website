Indicative self-check

Eight evidence questions. One clear starting position.

This tool runs entirely in your browser. Answers are not transmitted or stored. The result is a first-pass indicator only and is not an engineering assessment, estimate classification or regulatory opinion.

How to answerChoose the position that can be demonstrated from current source records, not the position the team expects to be true.
Question 1 of 8
Question 1 of 8Can the current cost basis be traced to source quantities, assumptions and rates?
Question 2 of 8Are the current P&IDs and as-built drawings reconciled to the asset?
Question 3 of 8Are structural condition, weight control and key temporary-state inputs verified?
Question 4 of 8Is the hazardous-material and naturally occurring radioactive material (NORM) inventory current, classified and traceable?
Question 5 of 8Do isolation and containment records support a credible make-safe scope?
Question 6 of 8Are inspection and integrity records recent enough to support method decisions?
Question 7 of 8Is the regulatory end-state and waste pathway supported by evidence and current capacity?
Question 8 of 8Are well plugging and abandonment (P&A), retained inventory and facility boundary assumptions clearly separated?
