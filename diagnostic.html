Liability self-check

Eight questions to identify where inherited information may be limiting confidence. A private, indicative self-check, completed in your browser.

Answer eight questions about the evidence

This tool runs entirely in your browser. Answers are not transmitted or stored. The result is a first-pass indicator only and is not an engineering assessment, estimate classification or regulatory opinion.

How to answerChoose the position that can be demonstrated from current source records, not the position the team expects to be true.
Question 1 of 8
Question 1 of 8. Can the current cost basis be traced to source quantities, assumptions and rates?
Question 2 of 8. Are the current P&IDs and as-built drawings reconciled to the asset?
Question 3 of 8. Are structural condition, weight control and key temporary-state inputs verified?
Question 4 of 8. Is the hazardous-material and naturally occurring radioactive material (NORM) inventory current, classified and traceable?
Question 5 of 8. Do isolation and containment records support a credible make-safe scope?
Question 6 of 8. Are inspection and integrity records recent enough to support method decisions?
Question 7 of 8. Is the regulatory end-state and waste pathway supported by evidence and current capacity?
Question 8 of 8. Are well plugging and abandonment (P&A), retained inventory and facility boundary assumptions clearly separated?
