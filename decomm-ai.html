Scope and boundaries

Before a walkthrough

Does decomm.ai calculate the accounting provision?

No. It supports the engineering and asset retirement obligation evidence basis. Recognition, measurement and the accounting provision remain separate accounting judgements.

Can the platform be considered separately from consultancy?

Yes. decomm.ai is a separate platform offering. Its scope and the Endura consultancy scope are defined explicitly.

Do these examples show a client asset?

No. The public example uses illustrative geometry and synthetic evidence scores. It is not a measurement of an actual asset.
