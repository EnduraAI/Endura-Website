      01

      Comparative assessment and business case

      When this is useful

      A removal or closure option needs a documented basis for the next decision.

      Useful starting information

      Asset inventory, scope boundary, required end state, previous studies and decision criteria.

      Work undertaken

      • Frame the options and applicable assessment criteria
      • Comparative assessment or best practicable environmental option (BPEO) support where applicable
      • Compare safety, technical, environmental, regulatory, schedule and cost factors
      • Record the assumptions and unresolved evidence

      Deliverables

      • Options comparison
      • Assumptions register
      • Decision brief and next actions

      The assessment basis, specialist inputs and decision responsibilities are agreed for the actual scope.

      When this is useful

      An engineering estimate or revision needs a clearer scope and assumptions basis.

      Regulatory context

      Regulatory context and scope boundaries.

      The applicable framework depends on the jurisdiction and the asset scope.

      Commonwealth waters

      The Offshore Petroleum and Greenhouse Gas Storage Act 2006 provides the Commonwealth offshore framework. NOPSEMA identifies removal of all property as the base case and regulates compliance with decommissioning obligations.

      NOPSEMA guidance ↗ (opens in a new tab)

      Title surrender

      For a petroleum permit, lease or licence, the titleholder applies through NOPTA for Joint Authority consent to surrender. This title process sits alongside decommissioning obligations and NOPSEMA’s assessment role.

      NOPTA title process ↗ (opens in a new tab)

      State onshore regimes

      Onshore requirements follow the relevant state regime. In Western Australia, onshore petroleum proposals are assessed under the Petroleum and Geothermal Energy Resources Act 1967. Other agency assessments and approvals may also apply.

      WA petroleum framework ↗ (opens in a new tab)
